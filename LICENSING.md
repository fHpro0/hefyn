## hefyn Community

This repository, under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). It is free forever. Odoo uses the LGPL for its core; we deliberately use the AGPL so nobody can host a modified hefyn without publishing their changes. In practice:

- You may use, modify and self-host hefyn freely, including for commercial purposes.
- If you modify hefyn and offer the modified version to users over a network, you must make the complete source of your modified version available to those users under the AGPL.
- If you distribute hefyn (modified or not), you must pass on the source and the same license.
- Using an unmodified hefyn internally, for yourself or your organization, adds no obligations beyond the license text.
- If you distribute a derivative work of hefyn, or let users interact with a modified version over a network, it must be licensed under the AGPL-3.0 too.
