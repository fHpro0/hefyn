id: KB-202
title: Returns, refunds and double charges
updated: 2026-09-02
---
- Returns: 30 days from delivery, unused or in original packaging. The customer starts the return in the account under "Orders". The return label is free inside Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
- Refund: paid back to the original payment method within 14 days after the returned goods arrive. Support cannot choose another method.
- Double charge: confirmed by the billing team only. Support tells the customer that billing checks it and answers within 1 business day. Support never says the money "will be refunded" before billing confirms.
- Goodwill gestures (vouchers, free replacements) are decided by team leads, never promised in a first reply.
- Invoice copies: available in the account under "Orders" > "Invoice". Billing sends a copy by email on request.
