id: KB-303
title: Delivery times, tracking and missing parcels
updated: 2026-07-21
---
- Standard delivery: 2-4 business days in Germany, 3-6 in Austria and Switzerland, 5-9 elsewhere in the EU.
- Tracking: the customer gets a tracking link by email when the parcel is handed to the carrier. It can take up to 24 hours before it shows movement.
- Marked delivered but not received: ask the customer to check with neighbours and the drop-off place, then wait 2 business days. Logistics opens a carrier investigation after that. Never promise a replacement before logistics confirms.
- Damaged on arrival: ask for photos of the box and the product. Logistics decides on replacement or refund.
