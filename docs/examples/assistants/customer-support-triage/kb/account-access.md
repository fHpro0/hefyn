id: KB-404
title: Password reset and locked accounts
updated: 2026-06-30
---
- Password reset: "Forgot password" on the login page sends a link valid for 30 minutes to the account email. Check the spam folder.
- After 5 failed logins the account is locked for 15 minutes. Support cannot unlock it faster.
- Two-factor codes: if the phone is lost, support can only help after the customer proves ownership through the verification form in the account help page. Never reset 2FA by email alone.
- Support never asks for a password, and never writes one in a reply.
