---
id: KB-101
title: Lumio lamp will not pair or does not appear in the app
updated: 2026-08-14
---
1. Check that the phone is on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network (5 GHz-only networks are not supported).
2. Switch the lamp off and on five times in a row (one second each) until it blinks twice. This resets it.
3. Open the Lumio app, tap "Add lamp" and follow the steps within 3 minutes.
4. If the lamp still does not appear: update the Lumio Hub to firmware 4.2 or later (app > Hub > Update), then repeat step 2 and 3.
5. If it fails again, support needs the lamp's serial number (under the base) and the app version.

Do not tell customers to open the lamp or to use a different power supply.
