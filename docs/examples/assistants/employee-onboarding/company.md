# Nordwerk GmbH, facts for onboarding

- Offices: Hamburg (Speicherstraße 14, 5th floor, reception opens 08:30) and Munich (Leopoldstraße 88, 2nd floor). Remote hires work from home in Germany.
- Core hours 10:00-15:00. Standard working time 40 hours per week, flexible within the core hours.
- Probation: 6 months unless the offer says otherwise.
- Hardware: laptops are ordered by IT at least 10 business days before the start; for remote hires the laptop is shipped to arrive the working day before the start. Software and hardware not in the request come later through the IT portal.
- Contacts: HR `hr@nordwerk.example`, IT service desk `it-help@nordwerk.example` (Slack `#it-help`), payroll `payroll@nordwerk.example`.
- Day one: all new hires attend "Welcome to Nordwerk" at 14:00 (Hamburg, room Elbe) or by video (Munich, remote).
- Security training (30 minutes, in the learning portal) must be done within the first week; the security awareness certificate is required before getting production-adjacent access.
- Buddy: a colleague from the same team who is not the manager; answers questions for the first 3 months.
