# Sales account executive, 30/60/90

## First 30 days: learn the product and the customers
- Complete security training (week 1) and the product certification in the learning portal.
- Shadow 10 customer calls (recorded in Gong) with two senior colleagues.
- Learn the pricing model and discount rules; pricing is only quoted from approved sheets.
- Receive the starter account list from the Head of sales.
- Meet: manager (weekly 1:1), buddy, sales engineer, customer success lead, marketing contact.

## Days 31-60: start selling
- Run discovery calls on your own with a colleague listening in.
- Create and maintain every opportunity in Salesforce within one day of the call.
- Send the first two quotes, reviewed by the manager before they go out.
- Get DocuSign sender access once the manager confirms (see access matrix).

## Days 61-90: own a pipeline
- Reach the pipeline target agreed with the manager (a number in the manager's plan, not in this file).
- Present a account plan for the three largest accounts.
- Probation feedback talk in month 3 with manager and HR.

## Tools
Salesforce, HubSpot (read-only), Gong, DocuSign (later), 1Password.
