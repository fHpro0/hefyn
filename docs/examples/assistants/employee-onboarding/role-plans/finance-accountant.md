# Finance accountant, 30/60/90

## First 30 days: learn the process
- Complete security training (week 1) and the finance four-eyes-rule briefing.
- Shadow the month-end close with the senior accountant.
- Learn the chart of accounts, the approval matrix for invoices and the cost-center structure.
- Book supplier invoices under review of the senior accountant.
- Meet: Head of finance (weekly 1:1), buddy, accounts payable lead, controlling, payroll.

## Days 31-60: take over a ledger
- Own accounts payable for a defined set of suppliers.
- Reconcile two bank accounts under review.
- Get expense approver rights once the Head of finance confirms (see access matrix).

## Days 61-90: close and improve
- Run one month-end task list end to end with a second person reviewing.
- Propose one process improvement in writing.
- Probation feedback talk in month 3. Banking portal view access is decided only after probation.

## Tools
DATEV Unternehmen online, SAP supplier master data (read-only), expense tool, 1Password.
