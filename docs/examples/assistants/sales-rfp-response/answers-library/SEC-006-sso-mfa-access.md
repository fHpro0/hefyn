Customers can sign in with SAML 2.0 single sign-on (Enterprise plan) or with email and password plus TOTP-based multi-factor authentication. Access inside the product is role-based with the roles Owner, Admin, Analyst and Viewer. Our staff use SSO with hardware security keys; access to production data is limited, approved and logged.
